About Chris Granger Mortgage

Chris Granger, NMLS #952015 Updated September 22, 2026

Chris Granger Mortgage is an independent mortgage broker that shops 100+ wholesale lenders to find the right home loan for Bay Area homebuyers, real estate investors, and homeowners refinancing.

Based in Alameda, CA. Licensed in eight states. Brokering loans since 2012, now through Xpert Home Lending, Inc., with 1,000+ loans closed and 290+ five-star reviews.

What Chris Granger Mortgage does

Home purchase loans

Conventional, FHA, VA, and jumbo financing for first-time buyers through $3M+ purchases. The outcome: a pre-approval listing agents trust, and a loan chosen from the whole wholesale market, not one bank's rate sheet.

Refinancing

Rate-and-term and cash-out refinances, with the break-even math shown up front. If a refi doesn't pay for itself, you'll hear that plainly.

Investor and non-QM loans

Financing for rentals, 2 to 4 unit properties, self-employed borrowers, and files that don't fit a big bank's box. Non-QM options open doors when standard income documentation doesn't tell the whole story.

Mortgage Blueprint

A free, interactive calculator that maps every dollar of a loan: payment breakdown, cash to close, tax savings, amortization, and side-by-side comparisons. Clients walk into offers knowing their numbers cold. Try Mortgage Blueprint.

Mortgage education

The Learn center and the weekly Three Point Thursday newsletter break down rates, programs, and Bay Area market moves in plain English.

How Chris Granger Mortgage works

  1. Intro call. Book on Calendly or call/text (415) 987-8489. Goals, timeline, and a first look at the numbers.
  2. Your Blueprint. A custom Mortgage Blueprint with payment, cash to close, and side-by-side options.
  3. Pre-approval. Full review of income, assets, and credit up front. Not a rubber stamp.
  4. Shop and lock. Your loan is priced across the wholesale market, and you pick the option and lock when it makes sense.
  5. Process to close. Appraisal, title, and underwriting, with milestone updates to you and your agent. A typical purchase closes in 15 to 30 days from accepted offer.

Who you'll work with: a licensed loan officer (Chris or Peter) from start to finish, with Gina coordinating documents and updates. How to reach us: phone, text, or email at cgranger@xperthomelending.com.

Frequently asked questions

Is Chris Granger a bank or a mortgage broker?

A broker. Chris is licensed (NMLS #952015) and works through Xpert Home Lending, Inc., shopping your loan across 100+ wholesale lenders instead of offering one bank's products.

How does a mortgage broker get paid?

Broker compensation is usually paid by the lender and is disclosed on your Loan Estimate before you commit. You see every dollar of it, and Mortgage Blueprint shows it next to your rate, fees, and payment.

Does it cost anything to talk to Chris or build a Blueprint?

No. The intro call and your Blueprint are free, and there is no contract or retainer. You only move forward if the numbers make sense for you.

Which states is Chris Granger licensed in?

California, Oregon, Washington, Nevada, Arizona, Colorado, Hawaii, and Texas. Most clients are in Alameda, San Francisco, and the greater Bay Area.

Who will I actually work with?

Your licensed loan officer is Chris Granger (NMLS #952015) or Peter Noerr (NMLS #2314358). Gina Navarro, the team's loan officer assistant, keeps documents, scheduling, and status updates moving.

How long does it take to close a purchase?

A typical purchase closes in 15 to 30 days from accepted offer, and refinances in 20 to 30 days. Appraisal scheduling, title, and underwriting conditions drive most of the variation.

What is Mortgage Blueprint?

Mortgage Blueprint is the calculator Chris built from the spreadsheet he used with clients for years. It maps payment, cash to close, tax savings, amortization, and side-by-side loan comparisons, and it is free at blueprint.realstack.app.
